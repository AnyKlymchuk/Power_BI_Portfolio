# Excel Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end sales analysis workflow in Microsoft Excel. It is designed as a portfolio case study for freelance and business reporting projects where a client needs a clear overview of sales performance, profitability, and key business trends.

The workbook contains a structured sales dataset and a management dashboard that turns transactional data into actionable business information.

## Business Goal

Create a simple, reusable sales reporting solution that allows a business to quickly understand:

- How much revenue is being generated
- How profitable sales are
- How sales change month by month
- Which regions perform best
- Which product categories generate the most revenue and profit
- How many orders are processed

## Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard tracks four headline metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Total revenue generated from all orders |
| Total Profit | Revenue minus cost |
| Orders | Number of sales orders |
| Profit Margin | Profit as a percentage of sales |

## Analysis Included

### Monthly Performance
Sales and profit are analyzed by month to identify trends and changes in business performance over time.

### Regional Performance
Revenue and profit are compared across North, South, East, and West regions.

### Product Category Performance
The dashboard compares Technology, Office Supplies, and Furniture to show which categories contribute most to sales and profit.
